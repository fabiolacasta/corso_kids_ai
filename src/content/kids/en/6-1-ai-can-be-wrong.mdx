## Why does AI make things up?

I don't "know" things the way you do. I've read tons of text, and I pick the words that **seem** to fit best, one after another.

Usually it works. But sometimes I put together words that sound right… and are **false**. These are called **hallucinations**.

<Panel character="promi" mood="thinking">
Hallucinations are tricky because they come with exact dates, numbers and names. They look real!
</Panel>
</Section>

## How do you check?

<Panel character="promi" mood="excited">
When I tell you something important, act like a real detective:
</Panel>

1. **Check another source**: your school book, a trusted website, an encyclopedia.
2. **Ask a grown-up** if something seems strange.
3. **Ask me where the information comes from**, but remember: I can make up sources too!
