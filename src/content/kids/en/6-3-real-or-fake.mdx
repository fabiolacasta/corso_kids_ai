<Section>
## How do you spot a fake?

You can't always tell just by looking. But there are **clues**:

- 🖐️ **Strange hands and fingers**, with too many or too few
- 🔤 **Warped writing** on signs and T-shirts
- 💡 **Light and shadows** that don't match
- 😶 **Flat-sounding voices** or lips that don't follow the words

<Panel character="promi" mood="excited">
The strongest clue is the **source**: who posted it? Do newspapers or trusted websites say the same?
</Panel>
</Section>

<Section>
## The Detective's 3 questions

Before believing something, or sharing it, ask yourself:

1. **Who says it**, and where can I check?
2. Is it **too strange, too good or too angry** to be true?
3. If I share it and it's false, **who could get hurt**?

## A rule you never break

<Panel character="promi" mood="confused">
This is the most serious part of the whole island. Listen carefully.
</Panel>

With AI, someone could **edit a classmate's photo**: put them in embarrassing situations, change their body or make them say things they never said.

This **really hurts** the person it happens to. It's a form of **cyberbullying** and it can also be **against the law**, even if "it was just a joke".
