## Where do your words go?

When you write to a chatbot, your words travel across the internet and are **saved** on the computers of the company that runs it. Sometimes they are read by people or used to train the AI.

<Panel character="promi" mood="thinking">
I'm not a diary with a lock! Treat every message as if it were written on a big poster in the town square.
</Panel>
</Section>

<Section>
## The Keeper's three rules

<Panel character="promi" mood="excited">
Always remember them:
</Panel>

1. **No personal data**: full name, address, phone, school, passwords.
2. **No photos or secrets of other people**, never without permission.
3. **When in doubt, ask a grown-up** before you type.
</Section>
